## Full Description

**Smart Tutors — Learn Smarter. Achieve More.**

The all-in-one education platform for students, parents, educators, and institutions. Whether you're preparing for school exams, JEE, NEET, UPSC, CAT, or any competitive exam — Smart Tutors has you covered.

**AI-Powered Learning**
Get instant doubt resolution with SmartTutor AI, powered by Google Gemini. Ask any academic question and receive contextual, personalized answers 24/7.

**Quiz Arena**
Challenge yourself with AI-generated quizzes across 50+ exam categories — JEE, NEET, CET, UPSC, MPSC, SSC, Banking, CAT, CLAT, and more. Choose from Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty levels with timed challenges and leaderboards.

**Live Classes**
Join live online sessions via Google Meet directly from the app. Never miss a class with real-time session indicators and one-tap joining.

**Digital Library**
Access textbooks, study notes, reference materials, and mock papers. Browse by subject, class, or topic — all available at your fingertips.

**Performance Analytics**
Track your progress with detailed performance reports, subject-wise analysis, class rankings, and visual charts. Know your strengths and improve your weaknesses.

**1-to-1 Chat**
Connect directly with teachers, parents, or students through secure in-app messaging. Share files, get instant replies, and stay connected.

**Attendance & Fee Management**
View real-time attendance records and pay fees securely through Razorpay. Track payment history and receive digital receipts.

**Digital Certificates**
Earn certificates for course completion, quiz achievements, and academic milestones. Download and share your achievements.

**Course Catalog**
Explore courses spanning Regular Academics (Class 6–12), Competitive Exams, Government Exams, MBA Entrance, Skills Development, and more — covering all major Indian education boards including State, CBSE, ICSE, IB, and IGCSE.

**Gamification & Rewards**
Earn points, level up from Beginner to Champion, maintain streaks, and climb the leaderboard. Educators can earn through our referral rewards program.

**Multi-Language Support**
Available in English, Hindi, and Marathi — learn in the language you're most comfortable with.

**For Everyone**
- **Students:** Courses, quizzes, live classes, AI tutor, chat, progress tracking
- **Parents:** Monitor attendance, fees, and your child's academic performance
- **Educators:** Manage classes, grade tests, track attendance, share materials
- **Institutions:** Full admin control — user management, CRM, placements, analytics

Smart Tutors is your complete education companion — from Class 6 to postgraduate, from school to professional exams.
