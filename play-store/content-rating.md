# Smart Tutors — Content Rating Questionnaire (IARC)

This document contains the answers for Google Play's IARC (International Age Rating Coalition) content rating questionnaire.

---

## Violence

**None**

The app does not contain any violent content, graphic depictions of violence, or references to violence in any form.

---

## Sexual Content

**None**

The app does not contain any sexual or suggestive content, nudity, or references to sexual activity.

---

## Language

**None**

The app does not contain any profanity, crude humor, or offensive language in its content.

---

## Controlled Substances

**None**

The app does not contain any references to or depictions of tobacco, alcohol, drugs, or controlled substances.

---

## User Interaction

**Yes**

The app includes user-to-user interaction features:

- **1-to-1 Chat:** Direct messaging between students, educators, and parents within the app.
- **Quiz Arena:** Competitive quizzes where users compete against peers.
- **Live Sessions:** Live online classes with real-time participation.

Users must be registered and authenticated to use these features. No unmoderated public user-generated content is visible to anonymous users.

---

## Shares Location

**No**

The app does not collect or share any precise GPS location data. Any approximate location data is derived from IP addresses and is used solely for analytics purposes.

---

## Shares Personal Information

**Yes**

The app shares personal information between authorized users within the platform:

- **Educators** can view student academic data (name, attendance, test scores, enrollment info) for the classes they manage.
- **Parents** can view their child's academic records, attendance, and performance data.
- **Administrators** can view institution-wide student and educator data.

No personal information is shared with unauthorized third parties beyond what is disclosed in the Data Safety section.

---

## In-App Purchases

**Yes**

The app includes optional in-app purchases:

- Premium educational content (textbooks, notes, mock papers)
- Access to advanced courses
- Premium features within the Digital Library

All purchases are processed securely. No consumable purchases with real-world value are offered. No gambling or chance-based purchases are available.

---

## Suggested Rating

**Everyone (ESRB)**

Based on the questionnaire responses, the app is suitable for all age groups. The app contains no objectionable content, and all user interaction features are restricted to authenticated users within an educational context.

**Equivalent ratings:**
- **ESRB:** Everyone (E)
- **PEGI:** PEGI 3
- **USK:** 0 (Suitable for all age groups)
- **Australian Classification:** G (General)

---

## Rating Justification

Smart Tutors is a purely educational application. It contains no violent, sexual, or otherwise inappropriate content. All communication features (chat, live sessions) are designed exclusively for educational interaction between students, educators, and parents. The app is safe for use by minors under parental guidance, and the content is appropriate for users of all ages.
